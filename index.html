01 / Featured Case

Moscow Residence

Building a new consumer-facing real-estate brand and marketing platform for a major city-backed residential sales programme.

Project Brand creation & growth
Role Brand & Marketing Lead
Market Residential Real Estate
Location Moscow
Challenge

Turn a property-sales operation into a recognisable consumer brand.

The project needed a distinct commercial identity for apartments offered by the city — one that consumers would understand as a conventional residential purchase rather than as part of the renovation and resettlement programme.

The task went beyond advertising. It required creating a brand platform, a coherent market proposition and a communications system capable of supporting direct residential sales at scale.

Strategy

Build trust around the unique role of the city.

I developed the brand around a differentiated proposition: quality housing, transparent purchasing, integrated neighbourhood infrastructure and the credibility of a city-backed project.

The communications strategy moved away from generic real-estate promises and established a more confident, institutional voice — positioning the brand around the standards, scale and long-term development of Moscow.

Project Creative & Content Transformation

Challenge

Make real-estate communication worth paying attention to.

A large portfolio generated an equally large volume of communication: project launches, advertising, websites, brochures, sales materials, customer guides, scripts, newsletters and special projects.

The task was not simply to produce more content. It was to make the communication clearer, more distinctive and more useful — while giving very different residential products a consistent INGRAD point of view.

Content System

Turning content into a marketing discipline.

We reworked how product information was structured and written across print, websites, landing pages, banners, presentations, CRM communication and special projects — replacing bureaucratic language and generic category clichés with clearer propositions and stronger customer utility.

Creative Campaigns

Turning propositions into ideas people could remember.

Campaign work ranged from outdoor and radio to promotional mechanics, scripts and integrated creative platforms. The objective was not creativity for its own sake, but an idea strong enough to sharpen the product message and travel across channels.

Cultural Collaborations

Taking a property brand into popular culture.

Selected collaborations expanded INGRAD communication beyond conventional real-estate advertising and connected projects with globally recognised entertainment and consumer brands.

Product Storytelling

Giving very different developments their own voice.

The portfolio ranged from comfort-class housing to premium waterfront residences and suburban products. Product communication translated architecture, location, amenities and lifestyle into differentiated narratives instead of repeating one generic corporate formula.

Digital & Special Projects

Extending the story beyond standard campaign formats.

Landing pages, special projects and digital activations created focused environments for individual propositions while keeping the communication recognisably part of the wider brand system.

Product Marketing

Moving upstream from communication into proposition development.

For new projects, the work started before advertising: location analysis, competitive context, audience needs, product advantages and the strategic narrative that could organise future marketing communication.

A unifying Big Idea created a platform for image and product communication across developments. In the portfolio, this section will show the strategic context and how the idea translated into communication rather than claim sole authorship of the agency-developed concept.

Inside the Organisation

I also created an internal newsletter about business communications, turning practical observations, examples and communication principles into an editorial product for employees.
