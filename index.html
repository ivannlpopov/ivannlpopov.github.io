01 / Featured Case

Moscow Residence

Building a new consumer-facing real-estate brand and marketing platform for a major city-backed residential sales programme.

Project Brand creation & growth
Role Brand & Marketing Lead
Market Residential Real Estate
Location Moscow
Challenge

Turn a property-sales operation into a recognisable consumer brand.

The project needed a distinct commercial identity for apartments offered by the city — one that consumers would understand as a conventional residential purchase rather than as part of the renovation and resettlement programme.

The task went beyond advertising. It required creating a brand platform, a coherent market proposition and a communications system capable of supporting direct residential sales at scale.

Strategy

Build trust around the unique role of the city.

I developed the brand around a differentiated proposition: quality housing, transparent purchasing, integrated neighbourhood infrastructure and the credibility of a city-backed project.

The communications strategy moved away from generic real-estate promises and established a more confident, institutional voice — positioning the brand around the standards, scale and long-term development of Moscow.

02 / Case Study

Severin Development

Building marketing as a business function for a complex B2B engineering and construction company — from positioning and digital presence to product marketing, brand identity and sales enablement.

Project Marketing function development
Role Marketing Lead
Market Engineering & Construction
Focus B2B / EPC / Industrial
Challenge

Turn engineering capability into a clear commercial proposition.

Severin Development had deep engineering and construction expertise, but the market proposition did not fully communicate the breadth of the company’s capabilities or its ability to take responsibility for technically complex projects.

The task was broader than communications: structure the offer, strengthen market positioning, create a coherent B2B marketing system and make the company easier for clients to understand, trust and buy from.

Strategy

Position the company around the projects others struggle to deliver.

I developed the marketing approach around full-cycle responsibility, technical complexity, engineering credibility and demonstrable project experience — with EPC/EPCM becoming one of the central commercial propositions.

The system connected awareness, trust and action through a mix of digital, expert content, PR, sales materials, product communication and brand development.
